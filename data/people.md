# Pasta Lab people

Faculty are `###` blocks with a key/value list. Students are one bullet each,
`Name | Affiliation`, with an optional third field for a note. Wrap the name in
a Markdown link when they have a homepage:

    - Someone | Their University
    - [Someone](https://their.site) | Their University | co-advised with [Name](https://example.com)

Faculty keys: role, affiliation, department, photo, website, scholar, github.
`photo` is the file stem in assets/people/, without the style suffix. Each PI
needs all three styles present: <stem>-chibi-v2.webp, <stem>-lego.webp and
<stem>-minecraft.webp.
